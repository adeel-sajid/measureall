Information We Collect
MeasureAll does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

The app does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as:

Name Email address Phone number Location Contacts Photos or videos Personal files Account credentials

You can use MeasureAll without providing any personal information.

Local Data Storage
MeasureAll stores certain application preferences and data locally on your device using SharedPreferences.

This may include:

Favorite conversions Recent conversion history App settings Theme preferences Decimal precision preferences Last selected units or categories Other application preferences

This information remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

Offline Usage
MeasureAll is designed to work completely offline.

Unit calculations are performed locally on your device. The app does not require an internet connection for its core conversion functionality.

Your conversion history, favorites, and preferences are not transmitted to external servers.

Permissions
MeasureAll does not require access to sensitive device features.

The app does not access:

Camera Microphone Location Contacts Photos or videos Personal files SMS Phone calls Bluetooth

Third-Party Services
MeasureAll does not use third-party services to collect or process your personal information.

The application does not use user accounts, cloud storage, remote databases, or analytics services.

If third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Deletion
Since MeasureAll stores its application data locally, you can delete this data through your device's application settings or by uninstalling the application.

Uninstalling MeasureAll will generally remove locally stored application data, subject to your device's operating system behavior.

Children's Privacy
MeasureAll does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information, users can use MeasureAll without providing personal information.

Data Security
MeasureAll does not transmit your locally stored application data to our servers.

You should maintain appropriate security on your device to protect any locally stored application information.
