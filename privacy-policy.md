Information We Collect
Smart Calculator does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

The app does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as:

Name, Email address, Phone number, Location, Contacts, Photos or videos, Personal files, or Account credentials.

You can use Smart Calculator without providing any personal information.

Local Data Storage
Smart Calculator stores certain application preferences and data locally on your device using SharedPreferences.

This may include:

Recent calculation history
App settings
Theme preferences (Light/Dark mode)
Decimal precision preferences
Other application preferences

This information remains on your device and is not uploaded to our servers.

Offline Usage
Smart Calculator is designed to work completely offline.

Mathematical calculations are performed locally on your device. The app does not require an internet connection for its core functionality.

Your calculation history and preferences are not transmitted to external servers.

Permissions
Smart Calculator does not require access to sensitive device features.

The app does not access:

Camera, Microphone, Location, Contacts, Photos or videos, Personal files, SMS, Phone calls, or Bluetooth.

Third-Party Services
Smart Calculator does not use third-party services to collect or process your personal information.

The application does not use user accounts, cloud storage, remote databases, or analytics services.

If third-party services are added in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Deletion
Since Smart Calculator stores its application data locally, you can delete this data through your device's application settings or by uninstalling the application.

Uninstalling Smart Calculator will generally remove locally stored application data, subject to your device's operating system behavior.

Children's Privacy
Smart Calculator does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Because the app does not collect or transmit personal information, users can use Smart Calculator without providing personal information.

Data Security
Smart Calculator does not transmit your locally stored application data to our servers.

You should maintain appropriate security on your device to protect any locally stored application information.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
